Your first entry in 6 simple steps

1

Pick a giveaway

Browse by prize category or search for something you actually want. Start with a few contests rather than entering everything.

2

Read the official rules

Check who can enter, age and location limits, the entry deadline, the number of allowed entries, and whether a purchase-free entry method exists.

3

Open the official entry page

Use the contest's entry link and confirm that it takes you to the sponsor's actual promotion. If a link is broken or unclear, look up the contest on the sponsor's official website.

4

Complete your entry correctly

Enter accurate information, follow instructions, and finish any required confirmation steps. Submitting multiple entries when only one is allowed can disqualify you.

5

Save and track it

Use Sweeps Titan's My Entries and Planner tools to remember what you entered and whether an entry can be repeated daily, weekly, monthly, or just once.

6

Watch for results safely

Check the rules for notification methods and response deadlines. Verify any winner message through the official sponsor before sharing sensitive information.

Our best recommendations for beginners

  • Start with free-to-enter promotions. In legitimate sweepstakes, no purchase is necessary to enter or win where the rules require it.
  • Favor clear deadlines and official rules. If important details are missing, skip the promotion until you can verify them.
  • Choose manageable repeat entries. Daily-entry giveaways only help if you can keep up without violating limits.
  • Keep a dedicated sweepstakes email address. It can help you organize confirmations and potential winner notifications.
  • Enter for prizes you could actually accept. Taxes, travel, shipping, and other prize-related expenses may apply.
  • Use the tracker as a personal log. Marking an entry as completed on Sweeps Titan does not itself submit an entry to the sponsor.

Understand the most common contest types

Sweepstakes

Winners are generally selected by chance. Follow eligibility and entry limits in the rules.

Skill contests

Judges or scoring criteria may determine the winner. Your submission must follow the contest requirements.

Instant-win games

The promotion may tell you immediately whether an entry won. Limits and prize conditions still apply.

Daily, weekly, or one-time entries

These describe how often the rules allow entries, not how likely you are to win.

🚩 Watch out for sweepstakes scams

Never pay an unexpected "processing fee," send cryptocurrency, buy gift cards, or provide online-banking passwords to claim a supposed prize. A surprise message that says you won a contest you never entered is a major warning sign. Verify claims directly with the named sponsor using its official contact information.
