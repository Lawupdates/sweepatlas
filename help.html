Quick answers

What's the difference between daily, weekly, and one-time entries?

They describe how often sponsor rules may allow an entry. One-time means one entry for the promotion; daily and weekly limits vary by person, email, household or other conditions. Always follow the specific official rules.

How do I avoid re-entering contests?

Tap Save on listings you want to revisit. After actually entering on the sponsor's site, use Mark Submitted to record an entry. Use Not Entered and Entered views to organize your lists. Entries saved on one browser may not appear on another device.

Does Sweeps Titan enter contests for me?

No. We help you find and organize promotions. You complete the entry process on each sponsor's website; marking an entry is your record, not proof of sponsor acceptance.

Why does a contest link sometimes open rules?

Some promotions provide only a rules page or alternate-entry instructions. We show that when known. A link that loads is not proof a promotion is open or accepts entries.

Can I hide contests I don't like?

Use filters and search to focus on your favorite prizes. A permanent exclusion list is not yet available; the Saved, Entered and Not Entered views help reduce clutter.

Why are some prize values missing?

We do not guess a value. If no verified approximate retail value (ARV) is available, consult the official rules or sponsor information.

💸 Prize values and taxes

In the U.S., sweepstakes winnings can be taxable income even when the prize is not cash. Keep the sponsor's documentation and independent evidence of fair market value, and consult a qualified tax professional about your circumstances. If a reported prize valuation seems incorrect, document the discrepancy and ask the sponsor for supporting information. Sweeps Titan does not provide individualized tax advice.

IRS Topic 419 — Gambling Income and Losses and IRS Form 1099-MISC information are useful starting points.

🛡️ How to spot prize scams

Be cautious when someone requests payment to release a prize, asks for passwords or one-time security codes, pressures you into immediate action, or uses unofficial social accounts. Sweepstakes entry should not require you to buy something when a free alternative is offered by applicable rules. Verify the sponsor independently.
